Activity 1 — Classify the Contamination

👥 Groups of 3–4 ⏱ 10 minutes 📋 5 scenario cards 🎤 Group presentation 🏆 10 points
  1. Your facilitator will give each group 5 scenario cards describing real-world contamination events.
  2. For each scenario, identify the type of contamination — pyrogen, microbial, particulate, chemical, cross-contamination, or mycoplasma.
  3. Determine which USP chapter or guideline applies to each scenario.
  4. Suggest one corrective action to prevent recurrence.
  5. Present your findings to the class — 2 minutes per group.
